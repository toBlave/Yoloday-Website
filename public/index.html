Who we are

What we do

Yoloday is a Melbourne-based startup creating AI-backed IT solutions for online consumers. Our aim is to make products that allow users to leverage the benefits of AI without the hassle. We deliver on this goal by creating sites that are simple to use, with the look and feel of a traditional website but with the added benefit of ambient AI. In 2026, Yoloday is kicking things off with a travel-based itinerary planner and a trademark and domain name checker site.

Yolo Travel

Yoloday Travel takes a simple one-page questionnaire and transforms it into a full itinerary plan.

We know that prompts and chat boxes can be hard for some internet users to navigate, so we put the AI into the background, delivering the look, feel and functionality of a traditional website. This gives a wider range of consumers the added benefit of using AI to plan their trip without the hassle.

With Yoloday Travel you can plan and budget a trip at your own pace, tailoring your experience to your personal interests — and our AI assistant does the rest!

Branding

Yoloday Branding is a comprehensive trademark, domain and branding assistant. We understand how time-consuming and difficult it can be to create a business name and then research domain and/or trademark availability, so we built this product to do it all in one place. Users simply fill out some key info and our site will suggest a name to suit, check the domain and trademark, and provide an easy-to-interpret risk rating report.

On the spot availability search and ratings

A simple colour-coded rating system shows users the level of risk and coverage of similar domains or trademarks.

Branding and business name coaching

We can suggest brand names for any business, from e-commerce and beyond. Simply key in the business offering and our site will take on the creative process of suggesting a name to suit.
